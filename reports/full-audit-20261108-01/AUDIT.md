# Full audit, 8 November 2026 (reduced form)

**Scope.** Nine areas. Four were completed by fresh-context auditors in the workflow run (`integrity-local`, `protocol`, `stats-1`, `process`; findings in the workflow journal). Five stopped at the usage limit after saving scripts and outputs; the orchestrator wrote their `<area>/FINDINGS.md` from those outputs (`integrity-public`, `stats-2`, `claims` incl. the paper draft, `alternatives`, `earlier`). **Caveat:** the orchestrator built N7's preparation, report layout, verifier and PR #6 package; the auditors had no access to that history. **Verification:** six majors re-checked from primary records in the orchestrator's own code (`verify_orchestrator.py` → `VERIFY_ORCHESTRATOR.txt`).

## 1. Verdict

The record is competent and unusually honest: every hash from the seal to the public copies links up, every N7 statistic reproduces from `POINTS.csv` to 1e-12 in three independent recomputations, the sealed readings are scoped, and the stated limitations are real. A TMLR referee would most likely object to three things: (i) the N6 figures the paper compares N7 against were interpolated linearly across a whole epoch and are upper-biased by about the size of the N6→N7 shrinkage, which also undermines claim 4's "384→512 rise is mostly training progress"; (ii) p\* jumps in the first quarter after every epoch boundary and the matched readings sit just there, so S4's confirmation depends on the interpolation and the matched comparison also compares different exposure phases; (iii) the public record cannot show who approved and verified the runs, and all "independent" reviews were by assistants of one family.

## 2. Findings by severity (deduplicated)

**Blocker:** none. No label, hash link or published number is wrong as produced.

**Major**

- **M1.** N6's post-hoc matched contrasts (RESPONSE §4–5, `make_figures.py`, paper Fig. 3) are inflated by E2–E3 linear interpolation: on N7's draws the coarse rule adds +0.0059 (P1), +0.0032 (S1), +0.0041 (S4); transferred to N6, D4 ≈ +0.0064 not +0.0132 and D1 ≈ +0.011 not +0.0027. Reword claim 4; flag the N6 points and "effects smaller than N6's" in the paper. (earlier E-1/E-2, claims C-1/C-2) — **Confirmed** (V2).
- **M2.** S4's CONFIRMED label is interpolation-dependent: m06's target lies in 768's E3→E3.25 jump (+0.0224); the later bracket end gives S4 +0.0104 [−0.0003, +0.0211]. The bound applied to P1 was not applied to S4; RESULT.md's limitations omit the structured non-linearity. (stats-1 A3-01/02, alternatives AL-1, claims C-8) — **Confirmed** (V1).
- **M3.** Exposure phase: at the matched point 768 has revisited 19–48 % of the training set, 512 at E3 100 %; validation NLL rises E3→E3.25 in 24/24 arm-draws. A limitation to disclose, not a refutation: the reversal survives anchoring 768 at E2.0/E2.25/E2.5 and a sweep of common loss levels, 6/6 everywhere. (alternatives AL-2/AL-3) — Confirmed in part (quarter steps, V2); anchorings not re-run.
- **M4.** "Training NLL", the matching axis, is defined in no prose document. From the sealed code: a separate no-grad evaluation pass over all 16,384 training sequences after the readout update, `model.eval()`, per-sequence mean next-token NLL, unweighted. (protocol P2-01, stats-1 A3-04) — **Confirmed** (V3).
- **M5.** The pre-stated power (sd 0.0025, ">0.999") is far from the realized configuration: P1's sd is 1.94× the planning sd and its mean 0.69× the planning effect, the plan's own pessimistic cell (0.703). Not a deviation; the comparison is undisclosed. (protocol P2-02, stats-2) — **Confirmed** (V4).
- **M6.** Public verifiability: `verify_matched_run.py`, which alone attests 20/20 and 21/21 and the terminal hashes, is hash-bound nowhere; the approval records, M1/M2 decision records, builder-pin diff and tool, analysis exit receipt and M2 status result are not public. (integrity-local A1-01, process A6-01/02/03/06, IP-3) — **Confirmed** (V5, V6).
- **M7.** Distribution-free support is thin: P1's m06 is +0.00002 and S3's m02 −0.00014; as ties the sign tests give p = 0.0625. (stats-1 A3-03, stats-2) — Unverified by the orchestrator; found by two independent recomputations.
- **M8.** The public record does not say plainly that every review, read and draft was by LLM assistants of one family with the owner the only human; the paper's AI appendix is a TODO. (process A6-05) — Unverified.
- **M9.** Paper wording: "six independent draws" (shared validation set, pool, tokenizer); the abstract omits the hypothesis's post-hoc origin; §4.3's title generalizes beyond 1e-4. (claims C-3/C-4/C-6) — Unverified (text).

**Minor** (details in the area files): protocol P2-03–P2-12 (reading-text drift for untriggered outcomes; "owner decides first" clause unenforceable; weights divided by the float32 mean; S2's saturation field printed as "none"; stage caps unreported; input check added after registration; layout frozen after the losses existed); integrity-local A1-02–A1-05 (hand-saved provenance timestamps; `ANALYSIS.json` carries no code hash; no console transcripts); stats-1 A3-05–A3-07 (rounded power inputs; validation set of 512 sequences and S2 margins unreported; one-sided 2.5 % level unstated); process A6-07–A6-15 (MEDIUM fixes before telling the owner; no ruling on the 5 Nov Grep breach; recompute timing; "no retry" rests on owner records; small public inaccuracies; a deleted test record; the redacted `RESULT.md` cannot be byte-verified; a score adjusted to a total); integrity-public IP-1–IP-6; alternatives AL-4–AL-9; earlier E-3/E-4; claims C-5, C-7, C-9.
